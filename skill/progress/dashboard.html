When a proof runs

  • Proofs run on a copy and cannot modify the original.
  • Writes stay within the copy and scratch folders. Granted toolchain folders and caches remain readable.
  • No network in or out, and no access to this computer's local services (host loopback).
  • The environment is cleared, with scratch folders for home, temporary files and caches.
  • Time and saved output have limits.
